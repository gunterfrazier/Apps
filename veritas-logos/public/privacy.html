Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Veritas Logos is a free Bible study app with no ads and no paid features. This page says what it keeps about you, who else handles it, and how to remove it.

Without an account

Everything you make (study guides, notes, highlights, bookmarks and settings) is kept in your browser on your own device. Veritas Logos doesn't receive it. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.

With an account

  • What's kept: your email address and how you sign in, and the same study data listed above so it's the same on each device where you sign in. Files you attach to sources and any API key you enter stay in the browser where you added them.
  • Signing in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture. Veritas Logos never posts anything for you and asks Google for nothing else.
  • Where: accounts are run by Supabase, on servers in the United States. Only you can open your saved data.
  • Emails: sign-in codes are sent through Resend. They're the only emails Veritas Logos sends.
  • How long: until you delete your account. Deleting it, from the Account page, removes your account and everything saved in it at once.

Family profiles

An adult account can create profiles for children or teens using a nickname, age group, and color theme. These settings are stored under the adult account. The Family library does not ask for a child’s email address or date of birth. A profile can be removed from the Family library, and deleting the adult account removes all its family profiles.

Bundled story media plays from Veritas Logos. Opening a credited source or a recommended external resource takes you to that service and its privacy policy.

Other services the site uses

  • Hosting: Cloudflare serves the site and, like any web host, sees your IP address when you visit.
  • Fonts: the type is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • Licensed translations: when you read the NIV, NLT or NKJV, each chapter comes through Veritas Logos's server from API.Bible. As API.Bible's terms require, it's told which chapter was shown, along with a random number this browser keeps. It isn't told who you are.

What Veritas Logos doesn't do

It doesn't sell or share your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies. It's meant for adults and teens, not for children under 13.

Questions or requests

Write to privacy@veritaslogos.com. If this page changes, the date above will change with it.
