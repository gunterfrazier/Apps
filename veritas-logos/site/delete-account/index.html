Delete your account

This is how to delete your Veritas Logos account, whether you use it on the web or the Android app. You don't need an account to use Veritas Logos, so you can keep studying without one.

In the app

  1. Open Veritas Logos (veritaslogos.com/app, or the app on your phone) and sign in.
  2. Tap Account (the person at the top right), then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Yes, delete my account. It's deleted right away, on every device.

Open Account

Without the app

Email privacy@veritaslogos.com from the address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account: your email address and how you sign in
  • Everything saved in it: study guides, notes, highlights, bookmarks, memory verses, reading progress and settings
  • Your family profiles

Nothing is kept. Before deleting, you can back everything up from Library. Anything you saved only on a device, without an account, stays on that device until you clear the browser's data for the site or uninstall the app.

YouVersion

Deleting your Veritas Logos account doesn't delete your YouVersion account. To stop Veritas Logos reading your highlights, disconnect YouVersion in Account first.
