The short version

  • Veritas Logos is a free Bible study app, with no ads and no paid features, funded by donations. This policy covers the website (veritaslogos.com), the app at veritaslogos.com/app, its embeds, and the Veritas Logos app for Android.
  • You don't need an account. Without one, what you make stays on your device and Veritas Logos never receives it.
  • We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • You can delete your account, and everything saved in it, from the app at any time.

Without an account

Everything you make (study guides, notes, highlights, bookmarks, memory verses, reading progress and settings) is kept on your own device. Veritas Logos doesn't receive it. Translations and study tools you download for offline reading are saved on your device too. Clearing your browser's data for this site, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

With an account

  • What's kept: your email address and how you sign in, and the same study data listed above, so it's the same on each device where you sign in. Files you attach to sources stay on the device where you added them.
  • Signing in: with a code sent to your email, a password, a passkey, or Google. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by the sign-in service. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture; Veritas Logos never posts anything for you and asks Google for nothing else.
  • Where: accounts are run by Supabase, on servers in the United States. Only you can open your saved data.
  • Emails: sign-in codes are sent through Resend. They're the only emails Veritas Logos sends.
  • How long: until you delete your account.

Family profiles

An adult account can create profiles for children or teens using a nickname, an age group and a color theme. These are stored under the adult account. The Family library doesn't ask for a child's name, email address or date of birth. A profile can be removed from the Family library, and deleting the adult account removes all its family profiles.

Story pictures and media play from Veritas Logos. Opening a credited source or a recommended outside resource takes you to that service and its privacy policy.

YouVersion

If you choose Account, Connect YouVersion, you sign in on YouVersion's site, and YouVersion gives this device permission to read and add your highlights. Your highlights then come in a chapter at a time as you read, and highlights you make here go back to YouVersion. The permission is kept on your device. Disconnect it in Account at any time. The Verse of the Day comes from YouVersion's daily verse, without anything about you being sent.

Other services the site uses

  • Hosting: Cloudflare serves the site and, like any web host, sees your device's internet address when you visit.
  • Fonts: the type loads from Google Fonts, which sees your device's internet address.
  • Licensed translations: when you read the NIV, NLT or NKJV, each chapter comes through Veritas Logos's server from API.Bible. As API.Bible's terms require, it's told which chapter was shown, along with a random number this browser keeps. It isn't told who you are.
  • Embeds: when a church or other website shows a Veritas Logos passage, word or guide, it loads from veritaslogos.com, and the same rules apply.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account, then Delete account. Your account and everything saved in it are removed at once, on every device. You can choose to remove this device's copy too. Delete your account has the steps, including how to ask us by email if you can't sign in.

Children

Accounts are for adults and teens 13 and older. Children use Veritas Logos through a parent's family profiles, as described above. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Questions or requests

Write to privacy@veritaslogos.com. You can ask for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
