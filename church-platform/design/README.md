# The Our Faithful dove

The app's logo is a dove made from the letters of OFC: the **O** is the head, the **F** is the raised wing (its leading edge and two feathers), and the **C** is the forked tail. It carries an olive branch. Thin, even gaps separate the head, wing and tail from the body.

Colors follow Veritas Logos: the dove in its blue and the olive branch in its silver.

The app's icons in `public/icons/` are made from these: 192 and 512 pixel icons, a maskable icon with room for round crops, an Apple touch icon, a browser-tab icon (SVG and 32 pixels) and a white notification badge.
