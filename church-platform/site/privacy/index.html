The short version

  • Our Faithful Church App ("Our Faithful", "we") is an app churches use for giving, check-in, events, groups, lessons and more. It's free for churches and funded by donations.
  • We keep what you and your church put into the app, so the app can work. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising analytics.
  • Your church decides what its staff and volunteers can see, by role. The database enforces those rules, not just the screens.
  • Card and bank details go to your church's payment provider (Stripe, PayPal or Square). We never see full card numbers.
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time (Me, Delete my account), or ask us to.

Who's who

This policy covers the Our Faithful website (ourfaithfulchurch.com), the app at ourfaithfulchurch.com/app, church pages and tag links on ourfaithfulchurch.com, and the Our Faithful apps for Android phones and for Google TV and Android TV (Our Faithful Screens).

Each church that uses Our Faithful decides what it collects about its people and who on its team can see it, much as it would with paper records or any other church software. Our Faithful provides the service and keeps the information on the church's behalf. If you have a question about what your church keeps about you, ask your church first; we'll help them answer it.

What we collect

When you make an account

Your name and email address, and, if you add them, a phone number, a photo, your address and your birthday. You sign in with a link or code sent to your email, or with a password if you set one. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by the sign-in service; nobody can read them, including us.

What you and your church add

  • Which churches and campuses you belong to, and your role in each.
  • Things you post or send: prayer requests, chat and group messages, event and class registrations, forms you fill in, and replies.
  • Serving: teams, schedules, availability, and, if your church asks for it, a signed background check consent. The background check itself is done by whoever your church uses, not by Our Faithful.
  • Giving: the amount, fund, date and payment method type of each gift, so your church can keep records and send giving statements.
  • Family members you add, including children (see Children).
  • Attendance: check-ins, class attendance, and arrivals at a service.
  • Sermons, lessons, slides, songs, pictures, videos and documents your church uploads.

Things your device can share, only when you allow it

  • Location. If your church turns on arrival prompts and you allow location, the app checks whether you're at a campus around service times. It saves that you arrived at that campus for that service, not where you were. If you send an emergency alert from the app, your location goes with it to your church's safety team so they can find you.
  • Camera. For scanning QR codes, badges and tickets, and for photos you choose to take or upload.
  • Microphone. For voice messages and the push-to-talk radio on the safety team's screens, only while you're recording.
  • Notifications. If you turn them on, your browser or phone gives us an address to send notifications to.
  • NFC. On Android, writing or checking an NFC tag reads and writes only that tag.

Church screens and kiosks

A TV, kiosk or check-in station paired to a church gets its own device account. We keep its name, what it's set to show, and a heartbeat (when it last checked in, its connection and storage) so your church can see that it's working.

Tag links and church pages

When someone opens a church's tag link (ourfaithfulchurch.com/t/…), we count the tap for that day. We don't record who tapped it.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising or tracking analytics, and we don't build profiles of people for marketing.

How it's used

To run the app for you and your church: showing you your church's content, checking children in and out safely, sending the notifications, emails and texts you or your church ask for, keeping giving records and statements, and showing your church's team what their roles allow. We also use it to keep the service working and secure, and to answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell personal information, rent it, or share it with advertisers.

Who can see it

  • Your church's team sees what their roles allow. For example, check-in volunteers see the children they check in, including allergies and care notes; treasurers see giving; greeters don't.
  • Other members see only what your church makes visible and what you choose to share, such as your name in the church directory (you can stay out of it) or a prayer request you post to the church rather than privately.
  • The public sees only what your church publishes on purpose: its page, events, sermons and the like.
  • Our Faithful can reach the data only to run, support and secure the service.
  • Others, only if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Services we use

These companies handle information for us, only to provide their part of the service:

  • Supabase: the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: hosting the website and the app.
  • Resend: sending email from the app.
  • Twilio: sending text messages, for churches that turn texting on.
  • Stripe, PayPal and Square: payments, under your church's own account. They receive your card or bank details directly and follow their own privacy policies.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when someone on your church's team asks the app to draft discussion questions, a kids lesson or a sermon summary, the Bible passage, the lesson or sermon text it's working from, and their request are sent to write the draft. Member information isn't included.
  • Google, Apple and Mozilla: delivering push notifications to your phone or browser.
  • Google Fonts: the app's and website's fonts load from Google, which sees your device's internet address when they do.
  • YouTube, Vimeo and Google Maps: when your church adds a video or map and you play or open it, that service loads it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Children don't make accounts; their parents or guardians add them to the family, and church staff may add them at check-in. A child's record can include their name, birthday, grade or class, allergies and care notes, who may pick them up, a photo if one is added, and check-in times.

That information is used to keep children safe at church and to run their classes. It's visible only to the child's parents or guardians and to the church team members whose roles include kids check-in or that child's class. Parents and guardians can change or remove a child's information in the app, or ask the church or us to delete it.

If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting

  • We keep information while your account, or your church's account, is in use.
  • Leaving a church (Me, Leave this church) ends your membership there.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, tap Me, then Delete my account, and it's deleted right away. Or email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete it within 30 days. Delete your account has the steps. Records your church has to keep (giving and pledges for tax statements, ticket payments, children's past check-ins, safety reports, and sermons, lessons and media you made for the church) stay with the church without your account attached.
  • A church that closes its account can reactivate it for one year. After that, everything in it is permanently deleted, and its files are removed.
  • Removing a paired screen or kiosk deletes that device's account.
  • Backups are kept for a short time to recover from mistakes, then replaced.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules in the database decide what each person and device can read or change, based on their church and role. Payment details stay with the payment providers. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you and your church.

Your choices

  • See and change your profile in the app under Me.
  • Turn notifications, location and camera access on or off in your phone or browser settings at any time.
  • Choose whether you appear in your church's directory, and whether a prayer request is private.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under privacy law; we'll honor them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and tell church admins in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, requests or deleting your account: email privacy@ourfaithfulchurch.com.
