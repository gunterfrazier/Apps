Delete your account

This is how to delete your Our Faithful Church App account, whether you use it on the web, an Android phone or another device.

In the app

  1. Open the app (ourfaithfulchurch.com/app, or Our Faithful on your phone) and sign in.
  2. Tap Me, then Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account. It's deleted right away.

Open Delete my account

Without the app

Email privacy@ourfaithfulchurch.com from the address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your profile, contact details and private settings
  • Your family, including your children's information and authorized pickups
  • Your memberships, and your place in groups, classes and serving teams
  • Your prayer requests, messages, posts, form answers and registrations
  • Your notifications

What your church keeps

Some records belong to the church, so they stay with the church after your account is deleted, with your account removed from them:

  • Giving records and pledges, which the church needs for tax statements
  • Ticket and registration payments
  • Your children's past check-in history, and safety reports
  • Sermons, lessons and media you made for the church

A church keeps these for as long as it uses Our Faithful. If a church closes its account, everything in it is permanently deleted a year later. To have any of these records changed sooner, ask your church, or email us.

Before you delete it

  • If you're the primary account holder of a church, make someone else the primary account holder (Admin, People), or deactivate the church, first.
  • Stop any recurring gifts (Give), so nothing is charged after your account is gone.
